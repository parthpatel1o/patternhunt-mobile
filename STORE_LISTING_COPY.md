# Pattern Hunt — Store listing copy

## Apple App Store

**Name**  
Pattern Hunt

**Subtitle**  
Find your next crochet pattern

**Promotional text**  
Browse fresh crochet patterns, vote for your favourites, and save ideas for later.

**Description**

Find a crochet project you can’t wait to start.

Pattern Hunt brings crochet patterns and makers together. Browse a community ranked by real votes, or start a swipeable Hunt to discover something new. From amigurumi to wearables and accessories, find ideas for your next make and keep your favourites close.

DISCOVER YOUR NEXT PROJECT
• Explore the rank board to see patterns makers are loving.
• Browse by amigurumi, wearables, accessories, or other.
• Filter by the past week, past month, or all time, and find free patterns.
• Search for patterns and creators by name.
• Start a random Hunt, or sort your Hunt by newest or oldest.

SAVE THE GOOD ONES
• Upvote patterns you love and help the community surface great finds.
• Save patterns to your collection and organize them into your own boards.
• Revisit patterns you’ve saved or upvoted whenever inspiration strikes.

SHARE WHAT YOU MAKE
• Submit your own crochet patterns with photos, a pattern link, and free or paid details.
• Add a PDF to a free pattern so makers can open it from the app.
• Edit your submissions and follow how they’re doing with creator insights.

Whether you’re looking for a quick project, collecting ideas, or sharing a design, Pattern Hunt helps you find your next favourite crochet pattern.

Some features, including voting, saving, and submitting patterns, require an account.
